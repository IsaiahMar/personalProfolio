About Me

I started programming in my freshman year of high school, learning Python along with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I haven't stopped since. I studied Computer Software Engineering at Thaddeus Stevens College of Technology, then transferred to Morgan State University, where I'm now in my senior year. Along the way I've built everything from small browser games to a full Laravel application with role-based access, messaging, and volunteer-hour tracking.

What keeps me hooked is that a problem never has just one solution. I like breaking a hard problem into small pieces, trying different approaches, and landing on the simplest one that works. At Morgan State, I'm an undergraduate researcher in the Human-AI Experience Lab, advised by Dr. Naja Mack. Long term, I want to start my own software company.
